---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - First release: three Earth maps for Nauvis, Terra Nauvis: Europe, Atlantic and Old World, as
      map-gen presets and Map types at map scales 1 to 6.
    - Map scale startup setting: 1 to 6 tiles per reference pixel, default 6; each value's label
      gives the world size of every map at that scale. A world made from a Terra Nauvis preset keeps
      the scale it was created with; a world that uses a Terra Nauvis map type alone follows the
      setting at every load, so changing it moves the terrain of that world's new chunks.
    - Map-gen preset Terra Nauvis: Europe, listed right after Default: Europe's real coastline in an
      endless deep ocean. A world made from the preset keeps the scale it was created with.
    - Map type Terra Nauvis: Europe in the Terrain tab: Europe's coast and spawn on its own, with a
      one-time notice that the preset adds the rest and that a changed scale or spawn city setting
      moves such a world's new chunks. A later version never moves it: its other values are frozen.
    - The water slider leaves a Terra Nauvis: Europe world unchanged, cliffs and land included.
    - Map-gen presets Terra Nauvis: Atlantic and Terra Nauvis: Old World, listed after Terra Nauvis:
      Europe: the Atlantic world and the Old World with their real coastlines in an endless deep
      ocean, each from its own fit.
    - Map types Terra Nauvis: Atlantic and Terra Nauvis: Old World in the Terrain tab, each with the
      one-time notice pointing to its own preset.
    - Spawn city startup settings for Atlantic (Random, the default, or one of 50 cities) and Old
      World (Random or one of 33: EarthMaps' 30 with Prague at the real city, plus Frankfurt, Aachen
      and Moscow): the map moves so the city sits at the spawn, with a lake nearby and the starting
      ore on the city's own land, as on Europe.
    - A loud message when a save names a map expression this version lacks, and an info line when
      the scale or spawn city setting no longer matches a save.
    - Spawn city startup setting for Europe: Random (the default) or one of 40 cities. The map moves
      so the city sits at the spawn, with land around it, a lake nearby, the starting ore and the
      enemy-free start of the base game; the intro names the city and the world size.
    - Inland lakes startup setting, off by default: sparse random lakes across the land, kept off
      coasts and out of the start area. Off leaves the lake at the spawn city, the rivers and the
      real lakes of the map; a world keeps its value for life.
    - A warning at the start, and to each player who joins, when RSO is active.
    - A world that uses a Terra Nauvis map type alone gets the same start as a preset world: the
      intro names its spawn city and world size, and with RSO active it gets the RSO warning.
    - Other planets' surfaces never take the Terra Nauvis map, and adding the mod to a world made
      without it leaves every surface of that world as it was.
    - Rivers, straits and canals on every map at every scale: rivers grow with the map scale and
      carry a walkable shallow ford every 60 reference pixels; Gibraltar, Dover and the Great Belt
      are two-way straits; the Canals startup setting (default on) opens Kiel, Corinth, Suez and
      Panama, and with it off every isthmus stays closed. Ships can sail every river to the sea.
    - Earth climate on every Terra Nauvis preset: the far north frozen, Scandinavia and Russia cold,
      temperate Europe, and hot deserts where EarthMaps paints sand (the Sahara, Arabia, Central
      Asia, the American south-west, the Kalahari, Australia and the southern Andes). With Alien
      Biomes its tiles and trees follow this climate, temperate land keeps the vanilla tiles with
      six adopted Alien Biomes tiles mixed in, and palms read climate alone; without Alien Biomes
      the vanilla tiles follow it and the far north turns to dry, dead ground. Every climate zone is
      a patchwork of several tiles that changes with the map seed, and the moisture and terrain type
      sliders (with Alien Biomes also its temperature sliders) move it.
    - Marshes: the Pripet Marshes, the Danube, Po, Rhone and Volga deltas, the Wadden Sea, the
      Vasyugan Swamp, the Sudd, the Okavango, the Mesopotamian Marshes, the Everglades and the
      Pantanal carry walkable mud pools and shallow water along their open water, with Alien Biomes'
      wetland grass and trees when it is installed and vanilla's lush grass between the pools
      without it.
    - The sea deepens a few tiles offshore at every map scale, with a beach strip along the coast.
    - Great forests startup setting (default on): the 16 great forests of Earth, from the Black Forest
      and the Scandinavian taiga to the Congo and the Amazon, hold about twice the trees of the land
      around them, while trees still follow the climate.
    - With Alien Biomes, the base game's trees, rocks, decals and plants grow again on the vanilla
      ground of Terra Nauvis worlds, beside Alien Biomes' own.
    - Ridges startup setting, on by default: cliff ridges along 16 mountain ranges, the only cliffs
      on a Terra Nauvis map, with passes at their historic crossings, a gap at least every 400 km
      and none across the starting area; ore never lies on a ridge. The cliffs sliders do not
      change them, and a world started with cliffs at none gets its ridges back beyond the
      starting area.
    - Where a map's edge cuts through land (North Africa and Russia on Europe, the far east of
      Siberia on the Atlantic and Old World maps), the land sinks into deep water a little inside
      the edge along a wavy coast that changes with the map seed, instead of a straight cut.
    - Destroyed Europe startup setting (default off, needs Alien Biomes): from the Gironde and Brittany
      to Warsaw, the heart of Europe becomes Alien Biomes' volcanic ground, hotter toward the middle,
      its edges changing with the map seed; Britain, Denmark, the Kiel Canal and the mountain passes
      stay outside, and the start area stays normal. A random spawn city never lands inside the
      zone. Without Alien Biomes nothing changes, and the game warns at the start and each player
      who joins.
    - Exact coastlines startup setting (default off, hidden in this version): on a map it acts on,
      each new chunk's coast snaps to the reference wherever the noise misses it by half a reference
      pixel or more. It acts on no map yet, so it stays out of the settings menu until one passes.
    - City markers map setting (default off, changeable in a running game): the map view names every
      city of a Terra Nauvis world's map where it lies; switching it off removes them.
  Compatibility:
    - With Alien Biomes enabled every Terra Nauvis preset is offered: the palms read the
      climate alone, never the coast. A world made from a preset without Alien Biomes does the same
      once Alien Biomes is added.
