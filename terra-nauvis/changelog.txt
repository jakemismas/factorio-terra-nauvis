Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-30
  Bugfixes:
    - The game no longer fails to load when an Alien Biomes tile group that Terra Nauvis adopts is
      disabled.
    - The start ore guarantee counts land after Exact coastlines has written it, so a river cannot
      cut a start off from an ore it counted. This applies to new worlds.
    - The Exact coastlines drift line no longer says a Map type world follows the setting.
  Changes:
    - Corrected the Atlantic preset, tooltips and README where they overclaimed.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-30
  Features:
    - First release: three Earth maps for Nauvis, Terra Nauvis: Europe, Atlantic and Old World, as
      map-gen presets and Map types at map scales 1 to 6.
    - Regular ore on Terra Nauvis presets forms natural mineral fields: lobed outlines with satellite
      pockets, a few large fields among many small ones, gathered in provinces, with rich pockets
      instead of a rich centre.
    - Ore hotspots startup setting (default on): historic ore fields at their real sites, with about
      three times the base game's biter nests inside them, and offshore oil fields on deep water
      where the Oil Rig mod is installed.
    - Ore hotspot amount startup setting: 50, 100, 200 or 400 percent. Hotspots ignore the map's ore
      sliders, stay inside their discs at their real sites, and ordinary ore stays out of them.
    - Map scale startup setting: 1 to 6 tiles per reference pixel, default 6; each value's label
      gives the world size of every map at that scale. A world made from a Terra Nauvis preset keeps
      the scale it was created with; a world that uses a Terra Nauvis map type alone follows the
      setting at every load, so changing it moves the terrain of that world's new chunks.
    - Map-gen preset Terra Nauvis: Europe, listed right after Default: Europe's real coastline in an
      endless deep ocean. A world made from the preset keeps the scale it was created with.
    - Map type Terra Nauvis: Europe in the Terrain tab: Europe's coast and spawn on its own, with a
      one-time notice that the preset adds the rest and that a changed scale or spawn city setting
      moves such a world's new chunks. A later version does not move it: only those settings, Canals,
      Inland lakes and Ore hotspots do.
    - The water slider leaves a Terra Nauvis: Europe world unchanged, cliffs and land included.
    - Map-gen presets Terra Nauvis: Atlantic and Terra Nauvis: Old World, listed after Terra Nauvis:
      Europe: the Atlantic world and the Old World with their real coastlines in an endless deep
      ocean, each from its own fit.
    - Map types Terra Nauvis: Atlantic and Terra Nauvis: Old World in the Terrain tab, each with the
      one-time notice pointing to its own preset.
    - Spawn city startup settings for Atlantic (Random, the default, or one of 50 cities) and Old
      World (Random or one of 33: EarthMaps' 30 with Prague at the real city, plus Frankfurt, Aachen
      and Moscow): the map moves so the city sits at the spawn, with a lake nearby and the starting
      ore on the city's own land, as on Europe.
    - A start gets iron, copper, coal and stone on the land it can walk to, room allowing: where the start area
      holds less than half of a normal start's amount of an ore on that land (an island or
      peninsula start, such as Stockholm or Athens), a patch of it is placed on that land when the
      world is created. With RSO the check runs on RSO's own start, unless RSO's Ore in starting
      area setting is off.
    - A loud message when a save names a map expression this version lacks, and an info line when
      the scale or spawn city setting no longer matches a save.
    - Spawn city startup setting for Europe: Random (the default) or one of 40 cities. The map moves
      so the city sits at the spawn, with land around it, a lake nearby, the starting ore and the
      enemy-free start of the base game; the intro names the city and the world size.
    - Inland lakes startup setting, off by default: sparse random lakes across the land, kept off
      coasts and out of the start area. Off leaves the lake at the spawn city, the rivers and the
      real lakes of the map; a world made from a preset keeps its value for life.
    - A warning at the start, and to each player who joins, when RSO is active.
    - A world that uses a Terra Nauvis map type alone gets the same start as a preset world: the
      intro names its spawn city and world size, and with RSO active it gets the RSO warning.
    - Other planets' surfaces never take the Terra Nauvis map, and adding the mod to a world made
      without it leaves every surface of that world as it was, apart from the grey cliff art beside
      Alien Biomes.
    - Rivers, straits and canals on every map at every scale: rivers grow with the map scale and
      carry a walkable shallow ford every 60 reference pixels; Gibraltar, Dover and the Great Belt
      are two-way straits; the Canals startup setting (default on) opens Kiel, Corinth, Suez and
      Panama, and with it off every isthmus stays closed. At map scale 6 ships can sail every carved
      river to the sea; from scale 3 Atlantic carves only Europe's 22.
    - Earth climate on every Terra Nauvis preset: the far north frozen, Scandinavia and Russia cold,
      temperate Europe, and hot deserts where EarthMaps paints sand (the Sahara, Arabia, Central
      Asia, the American south-west, the Kalahari, Australia and the southern Andes). With Alien
      Biomes its tiles and trees follow this climate, temperate land keeps the vanilla tiles with
      six adopted Alien Biomes tiles mixed in, and palms read climate alone; without Alien Biomes
      the vanilla tiles follow it and the far north turns to dry, dead ground. Every climate zone is
      a patchwork of several tiles that changes with the map seed, and the moisture and terrain type
      sliders (with Alien Biomes also its temperature sliders) move it.
    - Marshes: the Pripet Marshes, the Danube, Po, Rhone and Volga deltas, the Wadden Sea, the
      Vasyugan Swamp, the Sudd, the Okavango, the Mesopotamian Marshes, the Everglades and the
      Pantanal carry walkable mud pools and shallow water along their open water, with Alien Biomes'
      wetland grass and trees when it is installed and vanilla's lush grass between the pools
      without it.
    - The sea deepens a few tiles offshore at every map scale, with a beach strip along the coast.
    - Great forests startup setting (default on): the great forests of Earth (9 on Europe, 14 on Old
      World, 16 on Atlantic), from the Black Forest
      and the Scandinavian taiga to the Congo and the Amazon, hold about twice the trees of the land
      around them, while trees still follow the climate.
    - With Alien Biomes, the base game's trees, rocks, decals and plants grow again on the vanilla
      ground of Terra Nauvis worlds, beside Alien Biomes' own.
    - Ridges startup setting, on by default: messy bands of cliffs along the map's mountain ranges
      (9 on Europe, 13 on Old World, 16 on Atlantic), the only
      cliffs on a Terra Nauvis map. A ridge wanders round its range, runs wider where the real
      mountain is taller, breaks into runs of many lengths and frays out at its ends, with passes
      at the historic crossings, a gap at least every 400 km and no cliffs across the starting
      area. The cliffs sliders do not change them, and a world started with cliffs at none gets
      its ridges back beyond the starting area. Beside Alien Biomes cliffs use its grey stone art,
      which sits on its ground as vanilla's orange brown does not, on every Nauvis world.
    - Mountain ranges read as highlands: big rocks thicken round each range, clear of its passes and
      of every start, the ground leans to dry dirt, and beside Alien Biomes the tops of tall ranges
      turn to grey ground and snow.
    - Where a map's edge cuts through land (North Africa, Russia and Anatolia on Europe, Chukotka on
      the Atlantic and Old World maps), the land ends in bays and headlands that change with the map
      seed, instead of a straight cut.
    - Destroyed Europe startup setting (default off, needs Alien Biomes): from France to Warsaw and
      from the North Sea across Switzerland and Austria to Ljubljana and down Italy to about Rome,
      the heart of Europe becomes Alien Biomes' lava, black ash, red rock and scorched red grass in
      mixed patches, its outline different on every map seed and red on the map view; Britain,
      Denmark, the Kiel Canal and the Carpathians stay outside, and the start area stays normal. A
      random spawn city never lands inside the zone. Without Alien Biomes nothing changes, and the
      game warns at the start and each player who joins.
    - Exact coastlines startup setting (default on): on Atlantic and Old World each new chunk's coast
      follows the reference on every continent alike, except beside rivers, straits and canals, in
      the map's edge band and within 24 tiles of the spawn, so fjords, bays, the sea's narrow
      channels and islets the noise cannot hold come back as rounded natural shores. The map
      preview still shows the noise coast; land made from sea takes the trees and ground cover of
      the land beside it.
    - City markers map setting (default off, changeable in a running game): the map view names every
      city of a Terra Nauvis world's map where it lies; switching it off removes them.
  Changes:
    - Rivers wander: they meander off their polylines, their banks stop being parallel, they widen
      toward the mouth and taper at the source, and the square turning basins became natural pools.
    - Rivers scale down with the map: at small map scales they are a few tiles wide, too thin for
      boats, and only the largest scale carries boats along every carved river.
    - The start lake and the Inland lakes setting's lakes have irregular shores, and inland lakes vary
      in size.
  Compatibility:
    - With Alien Biomes enabled every Terra Nauvis preset is offered: the palms read the
      climate alone, never the coast. A world made from a preset without Alien Biomes does the same
      once Alien Biomes is added.
    - Beside Alien Biomes a Terra Nauvis map generates faster around vanilla land: Alien Biomes'
      decoratives and trees no longer search their whole tile lists on every vanilla land tile. They
      no longer grow where vanilla land may lie, which removes about 0.3 percent of the decoratives
      in temperate regions and thins them along the edges of the temperate band, by about 7
      percent; no tile moves.
    - Beside Alien Biomes the ground follows Earth's biomes: deep green rainforests, golden deserts,
      pale sand and gravel across Central Asia, dry yellow steppes, cold grey Tibet, pale tundra and
      a white Greenland. Temperate Europe keeps its ground.
    - Beside Alien Biomes a Terra Nauvis map generates faster: Alien Biomes' land tiles share twelve
      noise fields instead of reading one each. The patches look the same and fall in new places.
