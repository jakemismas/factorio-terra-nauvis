This zip holds the mod's Lua source. The complete corresponding source of this version also covers
the build tools (tools/) and the input data (data-src/) that the mod's generated files are made from.
The repository is private, so a link would not reach you. Instead, the author, Jake Mismas
(jake@jakemismas.com), gives anyone who received this zip the complete corresponding source of this
version on request, as a zip of the repository at the version's tag, at no charge beyond the cost of
delivery. The offer stays valid for at least three years from the day you received this zip.

The ore hotspots' places and sizes are facts, listed with their sources in the repository's
data-src/hotspots/deposits-research.md. No dataset ships in this zip; that file's country tables quote the
USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 data release, a United States government work in the public domain.
